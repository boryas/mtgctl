Bragging rights. Every (pilot, deck) pair in the Legacy Data Collection's Challenge sheets is rated separately, so a pilot's Doomsday and Vroomsday are two entries. Matches replay in order of date and round; all pairings of a round update together. K is 40 for an entry's first 20 matches, 24 after. BT is a Bradley-Terry fit of the same matches on the same scale, ignoring order: when it disagrees with Elo, the entry's recent form differs from its whole record. peak is the highest Elo the entry ever held.
