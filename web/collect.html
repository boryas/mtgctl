On Windows: install the uploader once (recommended)

Your logs then upload by themselves every time you close MTGO, and you don't need this page again.

  1. Open PowerShell (Start menu, type PowerShell) and paste your install line:
    …
  2. From now on, start MTGO from the new Play MTGO shortcut on your desktop.

The first upload reads every match log you have and can take a few minutes; later ones take seconds. If you start MTGO some other way, your logs upload the next time you log on to Windows.

What it installs, and how to remove it
  • install.ps1 saves your uploader key and downloads the two scripts below into %LOCALAPPDATA%\mtgctl, adds the desktop shortcut and a task that uploads at logon, and runs the first upload.
  • mtgctl-upload.ps1 finds your logs and uploads what the server does not have yet; it notes what it did in upload.log in that folder.
  • play-mtgo.ps1 starts MTGO and runs the upload when it closes.

To remove it, run this in PowerShell, then delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\mtgctl folder and the desktop shortcut:

schtasks /Delete /TN "mtgctl upload at logon" /F

Any computer: upload from this page

Choose your MTGO folder and the page uploads what is new. Come back and do it again whenever you want your results brought up to date.

On Windows it is C:\Users\you\AppData\Local\Apps\2.0; on Linux or a Mac, the same folder inside your wine prefix. If you keep old session logs (mtgo-*.log) somewhere else, choose that folder too, afterwards.

What gets uploaded, either way: your match logs (Match_GameLog_*.dat) and, from the client's session log, the lines about your decklists, events and game states, plus one line an hour so they can be dated. Game states include your own hand, which is how the stats know your opening hands and draws. The rest of the session log stays on your computer.
